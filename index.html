National Innovation: MineAI Technology

MineAI Technology (SMC-Pvt) Limited stands as Pakistan's first native, national Artificial Intelligence initiative, representing a significant milestone in the country's technological advancement. Under Bilal Mehtab's leadership as Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO), the company has positioned itself at the forefront of artificial intelligence innovation within the Pakistani market and beyond.

The organization, accessible through the MineAI Official Platform Hub at getmineai.net, operates as the main AI website and platform, with a mission to democratize access to advanced AI technologies while maintaining the highest standards of security and performance. The MineAI Live Web Interface at chat.getmineai.net provides users with direct access to cutting-edge AI capabilities through a real-time Web AI experience.
